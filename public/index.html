How to play

Watch, choose, score!

  1. Watch the teacher sign. The teacher will sign one word at a time. Each round has up to 8 signs.
  2. Choose the matching answer. Tap the answer tile that matches the sign as quickly as you can. Keyboard users can press Tab to a tile, then press Enter or Space.
  3. Correct answers earn points:
    1st 10 pts2nd 9 pts3rd 8 pts4th 7 pts5th 6 pts6th 5 pts7th 4 pts8th 3 pts9th 2 ptsAfter 9th 1 pt
  4. Wrong answers earn 0 points. You get one answer for each sign, so choose carefully.
  5. When the round ends, you will see your grand total. Your points carry over to the next round.
  6. A new slide starts the next round. Keep playing until the teacher finishes the game!
